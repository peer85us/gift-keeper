Questions

Where are my cards stored?

Right now, on your own device — in your phone or computer’s browser. Nothing is sent to our servers. Use Settings → Download backup to keep a copy.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Gift Keeper works in your browser. On iPhone, tap Share → “Add to Home Screen”; on Android, tap “Install app”. It then opens full screen like any other app, even offline.

Can it check my balance automatically?

Stores don’t share balances with outside apps, so you update it yourself — Checkout mode makes that one tap. Each card also has a “Check balance online” link.

Can my kids use it?

Yes. Kids get their own profile and only see their own cards. Parents can set a PIN so kids can’t open a parent’s profile.

Can I cancel Premium?

Any time, in Settings — Premium or Ultra. You keep all your cards — you just can’t add more than 5 on the free plan.
