GIFT KEEPER AD — VOICE SCRIPT (about 28 seconds)

Read each line, then pause about 1 second before the next one.
Friendly and relaxed, like telling a friend. Don't rush.

1.  That fifty-dollar gift card from Grandma...
2.  where did it go?
3.  Meet Gift Keeper.
4.  All your family's gift cards, in one wallet.
5.  It reminds you before a card expires — so you stop losing money.
6.  Send a gift card to anyone, just by text.
7.  Kids can swap cards — and parents say OK.
8.  Start free. Premium is just nine ninety-nine a month.
9.  Never lose a gift card again. Gift Keeper.

RECORDING TIPS
- iPhone Voice Memos app is perfect.
- A quiet room with soft things around (bedroom or closet beats kitchen).
- Phone about a hand's width from your mouth, a little to the side.
- If you mess up a line, just pause and say it again — I'll keep the best take.
- Send it to the Mac (AirDrop works) and tell me where it is, e.g. Downloads.
