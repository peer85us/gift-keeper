HOW TO FILM
  - Hold the phone upright (vertical). Back camera if someone can help, selfie camera is fine too.
  - Face a window — daylight on your face, not behind you.
  - Quiet room. Phone about an arm's length away.
  - Film each shot 2–3 times. Don't stop if you stumble — pause and say the line again.
  - Wear a plain top (no logos or busy patterns).
  - Real gift cards: cover or blur the card numbers before filming — or I'll blur them.
